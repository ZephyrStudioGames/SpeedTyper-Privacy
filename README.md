1. Zero Data Collection (No Personal Data)
SpeedTyper Pro is a completely simple and straightforward typing practice app. We want to be 100% clear: we do not collect, store, track, or transmit any personal data from our users. We do not require you to create an account, provide an email, or share any personal information to use the app.

2. Local Storage Only
To save your typing statistics, test history, and app preferences, SpeedTyper Pro exclusively uses localStorage (local device storage). All of your data remains strictly on your own device and is never uploaded to any cloud or external servers. If you uninstall the app or clear the app's data, your local progress will be completely deleted from your device.

3. 100% Safe for Children
We care deeply about digital safety. Because our app is completely simple and does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it is fully safe and secure for children of all ages.
